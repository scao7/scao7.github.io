Teaching

Courses at Knox College and earlier teaching at The University of Alabama.

I teach across the computer science curriculum at Knox College, from a no-prerequisite computing course for all majors to project-based upper-level courses in artificial intelligence. My courses treat AI-assisted development with agentic coding tools as a first-class engineering skill, and students ship real systems.

  • CS 343 Neural Artificial Intelligence. A project-based course in which student teams build and commercialize real AI products, covering large language models and diffusion models.
  • CS 317 Artificial Intelligence. Search, knowledge representation, probabilistic reasoning, machine learning, reinforcement learning, and large language models.
  • CS 208 Programming Languages. Functional programming in Haskell, parsers and interpreters, culminating in students designing and implementing their own language.
  • CS 142 Program Design and Methodology. Object-oriented programming in Java and foundational data structures.
  • CS 127 Computing, Technology, and Society. Python programming and computing fundamentals for an all-majors audience.
