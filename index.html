About

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Knox College. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of Alabama in 2025, advised by Dr. Fei Hu and co-advised by Dr. Yu Gan (University of Maryland), and my B.S. in Computer Science from The University of Alabama.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, and extended reality (XR). I build systems that watch, understand, and re-render human motion: from holographic patient rendering on a single camera to vision-language models that reason about what happens in a video. Much of this work is aimed at accessible rehabilitation and health, and it is supported by the National Science Foundation.

I am also a strong believer in undergraduate research. At Knox, students in my group build real systems, publish, and present their work. If you are a Knox student interested in AI, vision, or XR, please email me at .
